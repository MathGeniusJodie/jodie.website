## The Accelerationist Luddite

### Why Be an [Accelerationist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accelerationism)?

I don't know the exact risk from artificial superintelligence, and neither does anyone else. But I do know the risk of doing nothing: disease, poverty, war, pollution, worms that burrow into people's eyeballs. Technology fixes things. We don't have time to wait, people are suffering and dying *now*!

Accelerating isn't reckless, it's the only responsible thing to do.

### Why Be a [Luddite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luddite)?

We're building the wrong things faster than ever. My washing machine has a touch screen, it talks, and it has "AI", and yet I still have to empty the lint trap by hand. People make apps to stop them from using their other apps, apps that track your sleep because your other apps make you too overstimulated to sleep.

That's not progress! The wheel. Writing. Soap. That's real progress.

[Enshittification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enshittification) doesn't have to be the price of progress.

The point of saving time is to have time. The point of productivity is to produce something. We just have screen time and we just produce value for the shareholders.

### Por que no los dos?

<figure>
    <img loading="lazy" src="https://static2.mtlws.ca/whynot.jpg" alt="Why not both?" width="359" height="264">
  </figure>

I don’t hate technology. I hate technology that hates me back.

I want tools that accomplish their purpose and then _shut the fuck up_. I want to do human things, in a world my brain evolved to comprehend. I want robots in the fields so I can tend to my beautiful garden. I want efficient infrastructure so that my life can be inefficient.

Technology can either serve human flourishing or flattens the human spirit. We can use the same hammer to build the future we want, and destroy the future we don't want.
