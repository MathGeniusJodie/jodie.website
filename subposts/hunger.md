## Hunger is the best spice

Eating is more enjoyable when you're hungry. It's painful to eat when you're full. This also applies to time.

Having infinite free time makes time have 0 value. For a while last summer I wasted my entire days and got nothing done. Then I returned to school and took on more contract work. I conterintuitively started spending more time on hobbies and started actually shipping. I would do more in two hours than I used to in two days.

Quitting your dayjob to focus on your projects could backfire. Make sure your meal matches your hunger, then you'll taste it.
